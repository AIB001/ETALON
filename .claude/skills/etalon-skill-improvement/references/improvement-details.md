# Improve the operation that limits this research

Use a slow or unproductive calculation, missed useful branch, unexplained result
or repeated manual work to identify a concrete improvement. Start with the useful
state, controllable operation, objective and current limitation. Read only the
applicable rules and experience; direct SDK use and an unregistered local method
remain valid. A good local algorithm is a useful result. Transfer is a separate
hypothesis, not a prerequisite for helping the present task.

Choose a mechanism for the problem. Fixed structure with changing state may allow
precomputed local operators or incremental updates. Repeated equivalent work may
allow a parameterized rule or reuse. A costly workflow may be decomposed into
compatible operations; a broad rule may need a narrower conditional replacement.
A branch selector needs observable input differences and attention to useful
branches it would skip. Preserve the current method and cheap reuse as genuine
alternatives; a selector can cost more than the work it avoids.

If the limiting mechanism remains unclear, `etalon-research-decision` can reason
from the observed differences to an actionable expectation. The
[action basis and normal feedback adapter](../../etalon-research-decision/references/action-learning.md)
can connect selected experience to that action and its ordinary result. A saved
decision can also supply [decision-backed candidates](decision-candidates.md).
Use whichever connection helps; do not add a fixed diagnostic or Probe stage.

When the improvement procedure keeps choosing uninformative follow-ups, revise
that procedure as well. The optional [mechanism revision](mechanism-revision.md)
connects a mismatched prediction to its local assumptions and runnable alternatives.
Use feedback to change the action representation or choose a discriminating
calculation; saving a negative experience is not the end of the research.

Change the actual algorithm, parameter binding, workflow or selection rule that
could produce the expected effect. Keep its uncertainty and the complete old/new
bundles, including supporting code. **S** is a scientific method, **U** is its
selection/combination guidance, and **G** is the improvement procedure. Preserve
their separate versions and actual use; the generating call is distinct from the
changed object's parent lineage. A new text or digest does not itself improve it.
Skipped skill content need not be defective when direct execution worked.

Try the relevant part within the authorized task, using existing component
execution and method contracts. Registration or promotion is not a prerequisite
for exploratory use. Prefer feedback from normal execution, reuse checks whose
dependencies and meaning are unchanged, and compare the same external quality
endpoint. An internal prediction match or a representation review alone is not
method benefit. When interpretation changes, retain the original counterexamples
and distinguish old-result reanalysis from new evidence.

Judge useful output and complete cost: formation, failed attempts, preparation,
execution, diagnosis and verification. Keep unknown costs explicit and nested
timings separate. Measured branch attribution is different from physically avoided
work; development cost may need future uses to repay. If choosing a cheaper path
could miss a useful result, use relevant saved comparisons or a bounded alternative
when that uncertainty matters; unexecuted alternatives stay unknown.

Let the result change the next action: use locally, revise the affected operation,
continue exploring, retain, withdraw or stop. Preserve conditions, unresolved
mechanisms and useful counterevidence; confirmation strength should match the
claim. A fixed comparison can support local benefit, while transfer needs an
independent task. Do not relabel a development trial or require every candidate
to complete a full factorial study. `TaskSplit.transfer` may be empty.

On recurrence or continuation, use the existing disposition and candidate to
avoid repeating finished work. Reopen a retained outcome only when the stated
condition or relevant evidence changes. [Opportunity continuity](opportunity-continuity.md)
handles these optional records; no-change needs no invented candidate.

For machine-readable evaluation of saved results, use the optional
[evaluation contract](evaluation-contract.md); a passed rule supports
a scoped host decision. For bundles, actual use, cost and a later comparison,
use [revision records](revision.md). For worksets, partial use or
composition, use [selection and composition](selection-composition.md).
For revising G or recording the generator and its use separately, use
[self-revision](self-revision.md). These interfaces preserve learning
and continuation without making record creation a stage of every research action.
