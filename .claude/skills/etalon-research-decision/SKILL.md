# Make a research decision testable

Use the existing research graph, methods and evidence. This skill is optional;
simple reading, execution or a settled decision need not expand into a new study.
See [SDK entrypoints and boundaries](references/contracts.md) for the optional
interfaces, including information timing when the claim depends on what
information preceded the choice.

First identify the decision that new evidence could change. Describe its problem
structure: objective, subject, possible intervention, observable and constraints.
Scientific importance and the system's ability to solve it are separate judgments.
Keep unsupported dimensions unknown; a familiar target name supplies neither.

Offer competing explanations or options only where useful. They may coexist,
and an unlisted cause or inadequate measurement may remain possible. Bind support
and counterevidence to exact originals. Record concise scientific reasons and
observable predictions, not an invented transcript of internal reasoning.

For a proposed action, state which alternatives its possible outcomes distinguish
and what decision would follow. Unknown predictions stay unknown. A larger
computation is useful when it answers a remaining question, not because it is
expensive. Search, Judge, Quick and Deep can be revisited, skipped or ended.

Ground any intervention through the existing scientific-input and method
contracts: exact input identity, parameter binding, fixed quality criteria and
actual consumption. A free-text hypothesis does not set a force constant. Keep
chemical state, molecular graph, pose, receptor frame and preparation distinct.
Preserve declared, compiled, executed and admitted checks separately.

When evaluating a decision prospectively, preserve the visible-evidence window,
selected workset and prediction before releasing the outcome. The controlled API
establishes its own publication order; it cannot prove arbitrary host files or
model pretraining contain no answers. A known starting pose is known information
even if a copy is later called a reference. Retrospective material stays seen.

Use normal execution to evaluate the choice. Planned work, an input check and a
generated summary are not execution receipts. Distinguish numerical convergence,
physical validity, the task's quality endpoint and scientific relevance. Compare
like objectives; a restraint penalty cannot be treated as the same score as an
unrestrained energy. Missing or failed runs remain in the accounting and gaps.

Update each prediction with exact observations and evaluation references. Mark
unresolved or untested alternatives accordingly; do not close them by narrative
confidence. Preserve prior negative evidence and resolve gaps with explicit
evidence rather than deleting them. A new interpretation is not automatic method
adoption. Use the existing RSI candidate, evaluation, experience and disposition
interfaces when a method or workflow should actually change.

Keep local lessons scoped to their conditions. Parameter settings, experimental
design patterns and general integrity checks can have different reuse ranges.
The read-only capability profile groups exact versions and declared case families;
repeated seeds and duplicate summaries do not establish independent transfer.

Publish meaningful progress through research-step/checkpoint/entry. A new chat
should read the latest decision and update, find the exact evidence, and know
which branches are complete, paused or genuinely pending. An incomplete brief is
not by itself a reason to repeat a completed comparison.
