# Research with ETALON

The host owns the research question and the next action. Use ETALON for domain
tools, durable bounded jobs, budgets and scientific evidence. You may inspect
evidence and stop, select or combine skills, use atomic tools directly, propose a
new method, or abandon a route when evidence changes. The skill catalog is not
the boundary of the research space. No complete research DAG is required.

Discover installed capabilities with `etalon_capabilities` and read only the
skill or reference relevant to the present question with `etalon_skill_read`.
Skills carry content digests; this guide has a separate usage-method digest.
Catalog visibility does not prove that a host loaded or used a skill. Preserve
the actually observed version and a brief selection or stopping reason when
recording methods; unavailable telemetry stays unknown.

Use existing campaign and workflow status before submitting work. Registered
executors and the existing reserve/execute/resolve/admit path enforce scientific
identity, costs and evidence. Fixed workflows and numerical active policies are
optional local methods. Their present APIs choose declared nodes or policy
actions; they do not provide arbitrary host-selected molecular actions.

Duplicate requests are retries of the same job, not independent replicates.
Timeouts and lost responses leave outcomes uncertain. Inspect saved receipts and
reconcile before replaying or releasing reservations. Keep failed results and
their real costs. Do not treat a successful tool call as an accepted scientific
observation or as evidence of efficacy.

Keep object knowledge, domain skills, usage methods, improvement methods and
host/model conditions distinguishable. A new file or digest is a version, not
an improvement. Skill or usage-method changes need independent subsequent
evaluation with explicit costs and applicability. Preserve historical evidence
and the original meaning of existing numerical policies.

Continue routine work within the user's existing scope and resource authority.
Respect paused campaigns and explicit limits. Engineering setup does not resume
scientific batches, authorize paid experiments or send CRO orders. Shared role
instructions do not establish held-out data isolation; actual access boundaries
and independent evaluation must support that claim.
